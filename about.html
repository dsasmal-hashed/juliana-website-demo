Started by clinicians. Built to help every hospital do the same.

Juliana grew out of a care program for medically complex inpatients at a leading academic health system. Clinicians built it, ran it and measured it for nearly a decade. Juliana brings that experience to other health systems, so their teams start with years of practice already behind them.

From one hospital's answer to a model others can run

Juliana began with clinicians who saw the same patients return again and again, and decided one team should be accountable for their care.

2017program founded
2,300+patients enrolled and treated
5disciplines on one team
  1. 2017

    A response to burnout

    Clinicians at the health system were stretched caring for the most complex patients across many services, with no one team owning the whole picture. They built one interdisciplinary team, accountable for these patients in hospital and between stays.

  2. 2017 to today

    Refined in practice

    Enrollment criteria, care pathways and the daily huddle were refined patient by patient, with more than 2,300 patients enrolled and treated along the way.

  3. 2025

    Juliana is formed

    Incubated at Hashed Health to bring the model to other health systems, built on the expert knowledge and know-how of the founding program and its clinical leaders.

  4. 2026

    Juliana IQ takes shape

    A shared platform to find the right patients, keep one plan for each of them and run the model the same way every day.

  5. Next

    Partnering with health systems

    Bringing the model to new hospitals, built with their teams and measured against their own baseline.

Our mission

Every hospital should have one team accountable for the care of its most complex patients.
