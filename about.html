Started by clinicians. Built to help every hospital do the same.

Juliana grew out of a care program for medically complex inpatients at a leading academic health system. Clinicians built it, ran it and measured it for nearly a decade. Juliana brings that experience to other health systems, so their teams start with years of practice already behind them.

  1. 2017

    A response to burnout

    Stretched by caring for the most complex patients across many services, clinicians at the health system built one interdisciplinary team, accountable for these patients in hospital and between stays.

  2. 2017 to today

    Refined in practice

    Led by medical director Michael D. McCann, MD, MBA, a hospitalist trained in internal medicine and pediatrics, the team refined enrollment criteria, care pathways and the daily huddle patient by patient, with more than 2,300 patients enrolled and treated along the way.

  3. 2025

    Juliana is formed

    Incubated at Hashed Health to bring the model to other health systems, built on the expert knowledge and know-how of the founding program and its clinical leaders.

  4. 2026

    Juliana IQ takes shape

    A shared platform to find the right patients, keep one plan for each of them and run the model the same way every day.

  5. Next

    Partnering with health systems

    Bringing the model to new hospitals, built with their teams and measured against their own baseline.

Our mission

Juliana exists to help hospitals build care around their most complex patients, the people who cycle through specialties and settings with no one owning the whole picture.

Our model, proven at a founding academic health system, brings a dedicated interdisciplinary team together around these patients, so they have continuity across hospital stays and are cared for with dignity. We aim to improve care for these patients, reduce avoidable hospital utilization and bed-days, and ease the strain on the teams who care for them.
