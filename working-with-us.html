Questions health system leaders ask

Not covered here? Ask us directly.

Which patients are best suited to the Juliana model?

Adults with at least two admissions in 12 months, at least three major chronic medical conditions and an elevated risk score. Juliana IQ applies the criteria to your data, and your clinical leaders set the final panel and confirm every enrollment. See who it serves.

How is this different from our hospitalists and case managers?

Hospital medicine and case management are organized around shifts and services, and they do that well. Juliana adds one team that stays with a defined panel across admissions, shares one picture of each patient, decides together and tracks the panel's results over time.

What do we need to commit?

A dedicated unit for the team's admitted patients and a dedicated interprofessional team with a program manager. A 20-bed unit typically serves a panel of about 1,000 patients, roughly 50 per bed. The founding program cares for about 850 patients on 16 beds.

Who does what between our team and Juliana?

Your clinicians care for the patients and make every clinical decision. Juliana brings the operating model, coaching and Juliana IQ, embedded beside your team (Operate) or on call when you need it (Enable).

Do we need an EHR integration?

No. Juliana IQ works alongside your EHR from secure files, such as an encounter file and the daily census. Your record remains the record, and your clinicians keep responsibility for orders and patient decisions. Live feeds are optional, later, and only if you want them.

What data do you need, and when?

Nothing for a first conversation. The design phase uses an encounter extract from your records under a data agreement, and Juliana IQ starts from that single file.

What results can we expect?

The results at a leading academic health system show what the model can do. Your own results are measured against a baseline from your records, agreed before go-live, with fidelity reported alongside. How results are measured.
